FASHION STORE POS — RIGHTS AND USE
Version 0.1.0 — 2026-11-21

1. LICENCE
The software is licensed, not sold. The customer receives a device-bound,
time-limited right to use the application under the plan recorded in the
signed licence file (.lic). Ownership of the code and trademarks remains with
the developer.

2. SCOPE
A standard subscription covers one shop, one active branch, up to five named
users, and the features listed in the signed licence payload. A branch or
user above that limit requires a separate licence or plan upgrade.

3. TRIAL AND EXPIRY
A device may run an automatic 7-day local evaluation followed by a 14-day
grace period. During the grace period selling remains available so a shop is
not stopped mid-day. After the grace period selling stops; reading reports,
backing up and reprinting existing invoices remain available until the licence
is restored.

4. DEVICE TRANSFER
Transfer to a replacement device is permitted once without charge during a
paid subscription. Further transfers carry the fee approved in
docs/RELEASE-DECISIONS.md. Each transfer requires a new signed licence bound
to the new device fingerprint. Database keys are never copied between devices.

5. DATA AND PRIVACY
All business data remains on the customer's Windows device. The application
does not send customer names, phone numbers or invoices to any external
service. The customer is responsible for encryption passwords, backup media,
access control on the Windows account and compliance with applicable data
protection law.

6. BACKUPS
The customer must keep encrypted backups on external media and test a restore
weekly. The developer is not responsible for data loss when no tested backup
exists.

7. SUPPORT
Support covers installation, activation, and reproducible application faults.
It does not cover hardware failures, Windows administration, network setup,
or recovery of lost passwords. Support hours and contact details are supplied
on the delivery sheet, not in this file.

8. WARRANTY AND LIABILITY
The software is supplied "as is" for the agreed subscription term. The
developer does not warrant uninterrupted operation or fitness for a particular
purpose beyond the documented feature list. Liability is limited to the fee
paid for the current subscription period. This clause requires review by a
qualified lawyer before first commercial delivery.

9. FEES
The approved annual fee and transfer fee are listed in
docs/RELEASE-DECISIONS.md. Fees are paid in advance. Taxes are the
customer's responsibility unless stated otherwise on the invoice.

10. ACCEPTANCE
By importing a signed licence and processing the first live sale, the
customer confirms that the hardware acceptance tests in
docs/ACCEPTANCE-RECORD.md were completed and that the tax and return
policies were reviewed.
